Mentor Behavior Type Indicator

A profile of how you respond to your mentee's emotions

How it works

Six short scenarios. Each one asks what you'd say or do in a real moment with your mentee. Your answers map onto two dimensions of how mentors handle emotion.

How you read Reader (R) ↔ Filter (F)
How you respond Validator (V) ↔ Solver (S)

At the end you'll get a two-letter type and learn how it connects to my dissertation research on emotion management in mentoring.
